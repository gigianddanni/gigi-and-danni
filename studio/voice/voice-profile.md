## What goes here once it's built

- **In one line** — a sentence a ghostwriter could act on
- **Rhythm** — sentence lengths and the pattern they fall into
- **Vocabulary** — the words you actually reach for
- **Punctuation & formatting** — em dashes, line breaks, caps, emoji
- **Point of view** — the I / you / we ratio
- **Argument shape** — claim-then-justify, or walk-then-reveal
- **Endings** — how posts land
- **Never** — the constraints, with evidence
- **What has actually worked** — filled in over time from logged performance

Every rule needs a quote from a real sample backing it. No quote, no rule.
